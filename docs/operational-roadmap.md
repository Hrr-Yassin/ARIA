# ARIA operational reliability and intelligence roadmap

The owner ordered these phases on 2026-11-17. Original Phase00–09 acceptance
texts stay in PLANS.md. The sole scheduling exception is the retained explicit
eight-hour decision; it does not certify endurance or operational readiness.

Phase10 verifies infrastructure through current boot/service/container evidence,
bounded readiness, isolated dependency-loss/recovery checks, finite restart
supervision and durable reconciliation without uncertain action replay. Preserve
owner databases, volumes, credentials, scope and one API process. Do not reboot
or break owner dependencies to obtain evidence.

Phase11 first freezes controlled cases, evidence, expected decisions, public
rationale rubrics and collaboration measurements. Score decisions, factual
accuracy/evidence support, falsifiable hypotheses/alternatives and actual role
contributions separately; retain misses and unavailable denominators. Then run
existing agents using real configured models only when their admission, usage
accounting and resource/budget gates permit. Scripted responses validate a
scorer or agent contract; they cannot establish model intelligence. Freeze model,
prompt/source/input hashes and sampling settings; isolate sessions and never
dispatch benchmark proposals. No private chain-of-thought is requested or scored.

Phase12 requires independently authored senior cybersecurity engineer answers
for the same frozen case packet, with assessor provenance and no exposure to
agent answers. Keep author-written answer keys separate. Compare paired decisions,
evidence-supported conclusions, hypotheses, disagreements, costs and limitations.
An identifier or assertion alone cannot establish human authorship or independence.

Phase13 requires a concrete supervised run card: exact controlled laboratory,
authorized host/ports/actions, owner/session, model and fresh approved accounting,
hard call/token/cost/time/dispatch ceilings, named supervision, stop criteria and
positive/negative controls. Existing expired proposals remain expired. Missing
approval, capabilities or accounting stops dependent live work. No public target,
new tool profile, Kali/VM/network/privilege change or paid allowance is implied.

Phase14 prepares the final 8-hours/day,5-days/week protocol: prerequisites,
schedule, resource and financial ceilings, human roles, evidence, availability,
decision quality, recovery, interruption accounting and exit/retest criteria.
P14-02 holds the deferred eight-hour requirement. The owner has deferred its
execution to final operational validation; preparing this protocol does not
authorize starting it now or claim a five-day operational result.

Each phase records measured facts, failed/blocked cases and missing evidence.
No readiness percentage or comparison with invented senior assessments is valid.
